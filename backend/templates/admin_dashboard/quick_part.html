7. What is the writer's main purpose?
A) to inform readers about a new law
*B) to persuade readers to recycle more
C) to describe a local business
D) to complain about the council
Put * before the correct option (or write = B after the question). Paper layout works too — 1 A) Please. with no question text — and you can add the key at the end: ANSWERS: 1-C 2-A 3-B.
