SITUATION
You are a member of a sports club. The manager has announced that the gym will be closed for two months for repairs.

TASK 1.1
Write a letter to your friend who is also a member. Tell them about the news, how you feel and what you plan to do instead. Write about 50 words.

TASK 1.2
Write a letter to the club manager. Explain how the closure affects you and suggest what the club could do for members. Write 120–150 words.

TASK 2
Some people think everyone should do sport every day, while others believe it is a personal choice. Discuss both views and give your opinion. Write 180–200 words.
